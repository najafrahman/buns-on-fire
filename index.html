What We Serve

The Full Menu.

01

WTF! — What The Flame

A smash beef patty seasoned with mustard, salt & pepper, pressed on a buttered toasted brioche bun. Loaded with lettuce, pickles, tomatoes, grilled onions & jalapeños, melted cheese and our signature Buns Sauce. This is the one that started it all.

02

The Knockout

Our crown jewel. Everything in the WTF, plus crispy bacon and a fried egg. Smashed beef patty with mustard, salt & pepper seasoning, grilled onions & jalapeños, melted cheese and Buns Sauce on a buttered toasted brioche bun.

03

The Hot Chick

A three-step marinated breaded fried chicken sandwich with bold, flavorful heat. Stacked with pickles, creamy coleslaw and our Buns Sauce on a buttered toasted brioche bun.

04

Wild Chick

Grilled marinated chicken on a buttered toasted brioche bun with mustard, pickles, tomatoes, lettuce, grilled onions, jalapeños and melted cheese. The lighter side of bold.

05

The Cheeseburger

Stripped back and iconic. A smashed beef patty with mustard, melted cheese and ketchup. Clean. Simple. Perfect.

01

Beef Slider

A bite-sized version of the WTF. Smashed beef patty with mustard, salt & pepper, Buns Sauce, pickles and melted cheese on a soft slider bun.

02

Chicken Slider

A mini version of The Hot Chick. Three-step marinated breaded chicken with hot sauce, coleslaw and Buns Sauce on a soft slider bun.

01

Seasoned Fries

Our secret in-house seasoning blend on crispy fries. The recipe stays with us.

02

Loaded Fries

Our seasoned fries taken over the top. Choose your topping — Hot Chicken, Ground Beef, or Gyro. Drizzled with Buns Sauce and hot sauce.

03

Chicken Bites

6–7 pieces of boneless three-step marinated breaded chicken, served over a bed of seasoned fries with Buns Sauce.

04

Mozzarella Cheese Sticks

Six golden crispy cheese sticks, molten on the inside, with a side of house marinara sauce.

01

Wings — 4 | 8 | 12 Pieces

Pre-marinated, then deep fried to crispy perfection. Tossed in your choice of sauce and served with our signature dipping sauce. Buffalo, BBQ, Honey Garlic, or Dry Rub.

01

Rice Bowl — Chicken | Gyro | Combo

Your choice of protein over farm-direct long grain yellow rice. Comes loaded with toppings, dual sauces and fresh greens. A full meal in one box.

02

Protein Salad Bowl

Skip the rice and go full greens. Chicken, gyro or combo over a fresh salad of lettuce, tomatoes, cucumbers and onions. Topped with our signature dressings.

03

Chicken & Waffles

Three breaded chicken tenders with four fluffy waffles, maple syrup and Buns Sauce. Sweet meets heat — and it works.

01

Banana Biscoff Pudding

02

Oreo Biscoff Pudding

The crunch of Oreo meets the depth of Biscoff in a smooth, layered pudding that finishes the meal on a high.
